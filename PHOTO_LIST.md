# Photo shot list

Every photo the site needs. Until a photo exists, the site shows a grey placeholder labelled with
the filename and the brief below. To add one, name the file exactly as listed, put it in
`assets/photos/`, and rebuild the site. No code changes needed.

No stock photos and no AI-generated images. Real trucks, real people, real Auckland.

## Before you shoot (phone)

- **Use the main camera (1×).** Don't zoom. Walk closer instead.
- **Full size, straight off the phone.** Don't send through WhatsApp, Messenger or email "reduced size":
  they shrink and compress the photo. Use a cable, AirDrop, or Google Photos / iCloud at original quality.
- **JPG, not HEIC.** iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible. Android shoots JPG by default.
- **No portrait mode** on trucks; the fake blur chews up edges. Fine to skip it for people too.
- **Wipe the lens.** Most "soft" phone photos are a fingerprint.
- **Light:** early morning or late afternoon, sun behind you or to the side. Avoid midday glare.
- **No other company's branding in shot**, especially the principal contractor's trailers, uniforms or signs.
- **Plates:** fine to show your own; avoid other vehicles' plates where you can.
- **People:** ask before you shoot, and only use photos of crew who are happy to be on the site.
- Take 10 or more of each and send the best three. Small changes in angle make a big difference.

## How each photo is used

- **Hero** fills the whole top of the page behind the headline, with a navy wash over it so the
  white text stays readable. Phones crop it to a tall slice from the centre, so keep the truck central.
- **Owner portrait** sits beside "You'll deal with the person who drives."
- **Coverage** sits beside the list of areas, days, hours and temperatures.

The site makes smaller versions for phones and laptops automatically. Always supply the largest original.
